The source manifest lists the amending regulation. In the obligation graph, the high-risk items under Arts. 16 to 27 take the point (c)(i) date, or the point (c)(ii) date for a system that is an Annex I product with no Annex III area; a system that is an Annex I product and also falls in an Annex III area takes the point (c)(i) date. The Chapter III, Section 5 items (Arts. 40 to 44 and 47 to 49) and the Chapter IX items (Arts. 72 and 73) do not take a point (c) date: point (c) names only Sections 1, 2 and 3, so the graph dates them from 2 August 2026 under Art. 113, second paragraph, sets them to review_required and gives each a note, 'Literal reading, confirm with counsel', naming the point (c) date that applies if they follow the deferral (2 December 2027 for every Art. 49 item). Whether they follow it is the separate PENDING row above. The Art. 50(2) graph item takes the Art. 111(4) date where the profile's placing-on-market date is before 2 August 2026 (see the Art. 111(4) row above). Not encoded: the new prohibitions themselves, in Art. 5(1), first subparagraph, point (ba) (realistic intimate or sexually explicit material of an identifiable natural person, generated or manipulated without that person's explicit consent) and point (bb) (material or performance within the meaning of Art. 2, points (c) and (e), of Directive 2011/93/EU, that is child pornography and pornographic performance, except where a 'without right' defence applies under national law), together with Art. 5(1a) and (1b). The prohibited-practice screen covers Art. 5(1)(a) to (h) only. The scope check's Art. 111 notes (legacy placement, significant change, and the 2 August 2030 deadline in Art. 111(2) for high-risk systems intended to be used by public authorities) are generic: they do not compare a placing-on-the-market date with the Chapter III application date that Art. 111(2) refers to.
